AI-written notes (optional)

Your Note of the Day can be written by an AI service instead of the standard hand-written notes. This is off by default, and only happens if you turn it on here.

What is sent each time you submit the questionnaire:

  • your age, gender, major, and average daily study hours
  • your answers to 8 of the questionnaire statements
  • the stress level estimated by the app's model
  • whether you listed a chronic illness (yes or no), so the note stays gentle, but never what it is

What is never sent: your name, username, password, height, weight, and the details of any chronic illness.

Where it goes: to Groq, an outside company that runs the AI model, not only to this app's server. Its servers may be in other countries, including the United States. According to Groq's published terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models and is not kept by default, although its logs may be kept for up to 30 days. Read Groq's data information.

The note comes back as a few sentences. AI can be wrong, and the note is not a medical diagnosis.

You can turn this off at any time, here or on the questionnaire page. When it is off nothing is sent, and any AI notes already saved for you are deleted. Data that was already sent cannot be taken back by this app.
